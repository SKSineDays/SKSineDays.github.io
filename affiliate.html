Program details

Commission

Earn $1 for each eligible paid monthly Premium invoice linked to your active Affiliate Code, including the first qualifying payment and later renewals.

Customer discount

Your community gets $1 off monthly Premium when they use your code at checkout, while the promotion remains valid.

Sharing tools

If approved, choose your Affiliate Code and receive your sharing link and approved SineDay assets.

Dashboard and Premium

Track your supporters and earnings in the Affiliate dashboard. You'll receive Premium while your Affiliate account is active.

Earnings vary and are not guaranteed. Payouts are currently U.S.-only, and commissions have a 30-day hold. Affiliate Terms apply.
