SineDay is a journal for people who notice that no two days feel quite the same. An 18-day personal wave gives each day a perspective, SineDuck gives it a face, and the journal gives the memory a place to stay.

SineDay doesn't predict the future or tell people what to do. It helps people notice what they're already experiencing — and remember their days differently.
