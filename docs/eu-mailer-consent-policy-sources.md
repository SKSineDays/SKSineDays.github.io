# EU independent email-consent age policy sources

Research checked 2026-11-18. These are GDPR Article 8 national thresholds for consent-based information-society services offered directly to children. They are NOT universal minimum ages for app accounts, proof of age, or a complete national electronic-marketing-law assessment. The separate SineDay account baseline of 13 is a product policy, not a claim of worldwide legal capacity or consent compliance.

## Caveats and implementation boundary

- Denmark changed from13 to15 effective2024-01-01. Slovenia is15; Croatia is16. Do not reuse stale commercial maps.
- All27 EU members are covered above. EEA/non-EU jurisdictions were not researched in this map; an explicit mailer availability hold is preferable to an invented threshold.
- Swedish guidance also calls for considering whether users13–15 understand the consent. Numeric age alone is not proof of valid consent.
- Age-appropriate notices, freely given/specific/informed consent, electronic-marketing rules and national contractual capacity are separate requirements. This research does not certify the whole product legally compliant.
- Account eligibility13+ must not authorize a higher-threshold mailer. Use separate purpose-specific decisions and policy versions. A mailer refusal alone must not suspend account access.
- No parental-authorization pathway is implemented by this map. Below-threshold applicants require a mailer-only hold/support path, not a claim that contacting support constitutes consent.
- Self-declared birthdate is not verified age. Store the resulting policy decision, not full DOB for eligibility. Existing separately disclosed profile/calendar rhythm functions are distinct.
- Existing child accounts and ongoing email subscriptions require separate review; this change must not be described as retroactive cleanup.
