Transition

The wave turns and reflection begins.

Day 9 is the turn of the wave. Outward momentum begins giving way to inward refinement. Transition is not a fall from the peak; it is the moment the direction changes and a different kind of attention becomes useful.

Notice what naturally wants to slow, end, shift, or be reconsidered. The value here may be in recognizing that the old direction has already given you what it could.
