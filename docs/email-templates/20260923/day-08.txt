Day 8 • Peak

Challenge

Resistance reveals what asks for strength.

Challenge is the edge of the peak, where friction can become easier to notice. That friction may be external—a hard conversation, a demanding task, a boundary—or internal, like impatience, doubt, avoidance, or the pressure you place on yourself.

The point is not to seek difficulty. It is to notice what difficulty reveals about your strength, your limits, and what matters enough to stay with.

A pattern does not have to be dramatic. It might simply be the recurring moment when you notice where you have been overextending or underestimating yourself.

Your experience may echo the theme, differ from it, or show no pattern. There is room for all of it.
