Day 14 • Trough

Inner Work

Quiet can reveal what motion kept covered.

The trough is not a bad day or a low point to escape. It is the inward depth of the wave. Inner Work names the kind of honesty that can become available when outward noise has less pull.

You may feel reflective, private, tender, tired, spiritually open, or simply less interested in performance. Let the day be what it is. The deeper value may be in what you can hear here.

Notice today

What truth becomes easier to feel when the outside gets quieter?
