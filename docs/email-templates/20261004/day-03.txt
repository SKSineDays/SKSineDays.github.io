Creativity

Imagination flows and ideas spark.

Creativity is the wave becoming expressive. Possibility may feel more visible here—not only as art or invention, but as new ways of seeing a problem, arranging a day, speaking honestly, or imagining what could be different.

You do not have to produce anything to meet this theme. Sometimes creativity is simply noticing the unexpected connection your mind makes before you explain it away.
