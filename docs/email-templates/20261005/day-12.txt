Recalibration

When the plan meets what is actually there.

You plan to put a shelf beside the door, then measure the space. It is narrower than you thought. The purpose may stay the same, while the dimensions, position, or plan itself change in response to what is there.

Recalibration invites that kind of comparison between expectation and experience. You might consider a plan whose details have shifted. What did you discover, and which part of the plan changed with it?

Has something you've learned changed the plan you had in mind?
