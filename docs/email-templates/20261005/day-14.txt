Inner Work

The thoughts behind an ordinary answer.

Someone asks how the day went, and you say, 'Fine.' Behind that answer might be a small disappointment, a funny encounter, or a thought you haven't finished. An ordinary reply can leave much of the day unspoken.

Inner Work offers a little room for those private details. You might be curious about what you kept to yourself and why. A thought can stay mixed, unfinished, or private while you take a closer look at it.
