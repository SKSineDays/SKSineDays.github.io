Connection

A shared moment, seen by someone else.

Two people can leave the same conversation remembering different things. One recalls the joke; the other remembers that someone waited for their answer. Hearing the second account can change what the first person thought happened.

Connection invites curiosity about that other view. You might think of a recent exchange and something the other person said that stayed with you. There may also be something about their experience you still don't know.
