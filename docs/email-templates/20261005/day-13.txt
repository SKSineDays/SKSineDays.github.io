Day 13 • Descending

Release

When your reasons for keeping something change.

An old ticket turns up in a drawer. Once it was too important to throw away. You might still want it, or find that the memory feels complete without the piece of paper. The same object can mean something different after a while.

Release invites a look at that changing attachment. There may be affection, relief, hesitation, or several feelings at once. You can be curious about your reasons for keeping something before deciding what happens to it.

Notice today

Do you still keep anything for a reason that has changed?
