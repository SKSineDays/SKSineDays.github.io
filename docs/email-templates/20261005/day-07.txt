Insight

The detail that changes your first impression.

A short reply can seem abrupt until you hear it spoken with a smile. One detail changes your reading of the same words. Insight can begin with a small correction to the story you were already telling yourself.

You might remember a time when new information altered an impression. What did you notice, and what did it change? There can still be unanswered questions around the part that became clearer.
