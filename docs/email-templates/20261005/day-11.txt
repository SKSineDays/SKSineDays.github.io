Day 11 • Descending

Integration

What experience changes in the everyday.

Someone once gave you time to finish a thought. Later, in another conversation, you find yourself leaving that space for somebody else. An experience has become part of the way you listen, perhaps without a deliberate decision.

Integration invites attention to what you carry into ordinary moments. It may be a way of asking a question, using a tool, or welcoming someone. You might recognize where it came from, or simply notice that you do it differently now.
