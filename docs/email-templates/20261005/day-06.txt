Balance

When two things matter at once.

You want to finish what you're doing, and someone wants a little time with you. Both things matter. From inside that moment, balance can look like a pause, a conversation, or a choice that still leaves something waiting.

This theme invites a closer look at what is asking for your attention. There may be two wishes of your own, or a need you share with someone else. You can describe the pull of each without settling it immediately.
