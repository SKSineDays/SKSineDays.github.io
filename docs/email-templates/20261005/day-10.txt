Day 10 • Descending

Reflection

A past moment, from where you stand now.

An old photograph holds a moment you once stood inside. You may remember what you were looking forward to, or notice someone at the edge who seemed unimportant then. The picture is unchanged; your account of it may have moved on.

Reflection invites a return to one moment, however ordinary. You can remember what you understood at the time and consider what you see now. The two accounts may agree, or leave a question between them.
