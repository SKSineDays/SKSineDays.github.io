Day 17 • Emerging

Foundation

What you count on in an ordinary day.

The spare key is where it always is. Someone opens the shop each morning. A friend knows the story without needing the beginning again. These familiar arrangements can become so much a part of a day that they are easy to overlook.

Foundation invites attention to something dependable in your life and how it came to be. It may have taken time, care, or repeated effort from someone. Looking closely can bring that person or history into view.

Notice today

What do you regularly count on, and who or what keeps it there?
