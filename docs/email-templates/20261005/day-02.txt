Momentum

Picking up where you left off.

A jigsaw sits on a table. Someone adds a few pieces, leaves it for a day, and comes back. Each return begins with what is already there, including the pieces that still don't fit.

Momentum invites a look at something that continues across separate moments. You might notice what you can pick up again, or how what came before shapes the next attempt. A pause is part of that account too.
