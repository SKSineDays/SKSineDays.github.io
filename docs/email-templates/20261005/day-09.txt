Day 9 • Crest

Transition

Being in the middle of a change.

A room half-packed for a move still contains the old life, though it no longer works quite the same way. The kettle is out; the cups are in a box. Much of a transition happens in these in-between arrangements.

You might consider a change while it is still under way. Some things remain familiar, and others need a new place or a new name. There is room to notice the awkwardness and ordinary details before knowing how it will turn out.

Notice today

Is anything in your life between a familiar arrangement and a new one?
