Healing

Care in an ordinary moment.

A meal left at a door. A chair pulled closer. Someone willing to wait while another person finds the words. Care often has these ordinary details, which can look different from the side of the person receiving it.

For Healing, you might consider one such moment and what the gesture meant to you. Perhaps it was welcome, awkward, or difficult to describe. There is room for your account of it, including what was still unresolved afterward.

Notice today

What does a small gesture of care look like from the receiving side?
