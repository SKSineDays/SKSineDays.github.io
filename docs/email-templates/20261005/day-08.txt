Challenge

What happens when something gets in the way.

The door won't open. Someone tries the handle again, looks for another entrance, or turns to the person beside them. A small obstacle can make the next few moments quite different from what was expected.

Challenge offers a way to observe those moments: the first reaction, the available choices, the thing someone tries. A difficulty may remain difficult. You can leave the account there, with a person still deciding what to do.

Notice today

When something gets in your way, what do you notice about your first response?
