Day 18 • Emerging

Culmination

Looking at what remains when something ends.

After the guests leave, the room holds the remains of an evening: a glass on the sill, a moved chair, something someone said. The gathering has ended, and those details offer a way to look at the whole occasion.

Day 18 closes this set of themes. Culmination invites a similar look at a stretch of your own life, large or small. You might name where it began, where it ended, and one thing that stays with you.
