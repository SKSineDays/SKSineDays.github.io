Day 3 • Ascending

Creativity

Another way to see a familiar thing.

A cardboard box can be packaging, a hiding place, or the beginning of a small theatre. Its possible uses depend on who is looking at it and what they imagine. Creativity offers that freedom to an ordinary thing.

You might look around for something you usually pass without a thought. A different use, a strange comparison, or a made-up history could change how you see it. The idea can remain a passing amusement.

Notice today

What familiar thing could you look at another way?
