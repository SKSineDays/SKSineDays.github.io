Day 1 • Rising

Initiation

The moment something gets started.

Before a conversation has a subject, someone says hello. Before a page has a story, there is a first line. Initiation invites attention to that small move from considering something to being in it.

You may have a beginning in mind, or recognize one only when you look back. There is plenty to notice in the moment itself: where you were, what you did, and what you couldn't yet know.

Notice today

Is there a small beginning you can put your finger on?
