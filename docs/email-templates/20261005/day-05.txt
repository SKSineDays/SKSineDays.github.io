Day 5 • Ascending

Productivity

What a piece of work makes possible.

Someone clears the kitchen table, and there is room to eat together. The task takes a few minutes; its purpose becomes visible when the plates arrive. Work can be understood through the difference it makes in a particular scene.

Productivity offers a chance to look at one piece of work that way. You might consider what it is for, who uses it, or which part usually goes unnoticed. Even something unfinished can have a story worth telling.
