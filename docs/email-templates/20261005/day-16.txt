Preparation

Getting ready for something you haven't met yet.

A bag waits by the door with a notebook, a spare layer, and an address folded into a pocket. Each item says something about what its owner expects. There will still be things they could not have packed for.

Preparation invites a look at the time before an occasion. You might consider what you gather, what you rehearse, or which question you hope to ask. The details can show both your expectations and the uncertainty you take along.
