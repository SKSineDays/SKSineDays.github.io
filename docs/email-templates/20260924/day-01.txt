Initiation

Fresh beginnings call you forward.

Day 1 is the first lift of the wave—the place where direction begins to take shape. Initiation does not have to arrive as a dramatic beginning. It may be a decision, a first sentence, a call you finally make, or the quiet sense that something is ready to move.

The card gives the day a name. Your experience decides what that name means. Notice what asks for a first step without forcing the day to become productive or important.
