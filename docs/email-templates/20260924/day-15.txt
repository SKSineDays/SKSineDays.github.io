Day 15 • Trough

Healing

Gentle care restores what effort has spent.

Healing is the trough becoming restorative. This does not mean something must be wrong with you. It is the part of the wave where care, safety, nourishment, forgiveness, and recovery may carry more meaning than pushing harder.

Healing may show up physically, emotionally, relationally, or simply as the relief of allowing yourself to be softer than usual.

Notice today

What kind of care feels especially meaningful?
