Day 6 • Peak

Balance

Harmony emerges through adjustment.

At the peak, the wave offers a wider view. Balance is not perfect stillness; it is the ability to feel what is pulling too hard, what is being neglected, and where a small adjustment could create more ease.

Your version may look like boundaries, rest after effort, a calmer decision, or the realization that two important parts of life need to coexist instead of compete.
