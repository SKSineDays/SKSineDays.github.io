Day 14 • Trough

Inner Work

Quiet can reveal what motion kept covered.

The trough is not a bad day or a low point to escape. It is the inward depth of the wave. Inner Work names the kind of honesty that can become available when outward noise has less pull.

You may feel reflective, private, tender, tired, spiritually open, or simply less interested in performance. Let the day be what it is. The deeper value may be in what you can hear here.

Notice today

What truth becomes easier to feel when the outside gets quieter?

Your Sine, through your eyes

What patterns surface in the quietest part of your wave?

When Day 14 returns, do the same emotions, memories, spiritual questions, dreams, body sensations, or needs for solitude appear? Do you notice recurring truths that are harder to hear during the rising half of the wave?

This is exactly where we do not want to fill the silence for you. We want to know what you find in it.

Your experience may echo the theme, differ from it, or show no pattern. There is room for all of it.
