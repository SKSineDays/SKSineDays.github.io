Meet SineDuck: a quiet visual mirror of the day.

An 18-day rhythm for noticing how each day moves through you.

SineDay maps your days onto a repeating 18-day wave. The wave moves through rising, peak, crest, descending, trough, and emerging phases—each with a different theme for reflection.

It is not a prediction of what will happen, and it is not a command for how you should feel. It is a language for noticing. The card gives you a theme. Your lived day tells you what that theme actually meant.

SineDuck marks your place in that repeating rhythm. Each day has its own duck, a theme to consider, and space for your own thoughts.

Each morning

Around 6:00 AM in your local time, your Daily SineDay email will name your place on the wave and offer a short way to notice that theme through your own experience.

You do not have to make the day match the card. Notice what is actually there. Over time, the interesting part may not be any single day—it may be the patterns that return when your wave comes back around.

Your Sine, through your eyes

What are you noticing in your wave?

SineDay can tell you where you are on the wave. Only you can tell us what that place becomes inside an actual life. We want the stories, but we also want the patterns.

As the 18 days repeat, notice what seems to return with them: a kind of conversation, a familiar thought, a need for quiet. Notice what changes, too.

It does not have to be polished or profound. Start with what is here. A single thought is enough; patterns can become clearer as you return.
