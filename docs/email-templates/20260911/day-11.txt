Integration

Experience settles into something you can carry.

Integration is what happens when experience stops being only an event and begins becoming part of you. Rest, quiet, repetition, and distance can all help the lessons of the earlier wave settle into a more usable form.

You may not feel like you are accomplishing much here. That can be exactly the point. Some growth becomes real only after the noise drops.

Notice today

What feels like it is settling into place?
