Recalibration

Small adjustments bring the wave back into alignment.

Recalibration is the quiet intelligence of adjustment. As the wave descends, what once seemed workable may need to be resized, redirected, simplified, or released from an unrealistic expectation.

This theme is less about changing your whole life than noticing the one degree of correction that makes the next stretch feel more honest.

Notice today

What needs a smaller, cleaner adjustment?

What patterns of adjustment keep showing up here?

When Day 12 returns, do you revisit schedules, expectations, habits, priorities, or relationships in similar ways? Do certain plans repeatedly ask to become simpler or more realistic at this point in your wave?

Those repeated corrections are worth recording. They can show where your life keeps asking for a better fit.
