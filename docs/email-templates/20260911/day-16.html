Day 16 • Trough

Preparation

The next rise begins as quiet readiness.

Preparation is the trough beginning to turn toward possibility. The new wave is not fully rising yet, but readiness can start forming through small acts of structure, rest, organization, and intention.

This is the kind of preparation that reduces friction later: clearing a space, gathering what you need, making a simple plan, or recognizing what deserves your energy when the rise returns.

Your Sine, through your eyes

What patterns of readiness show up near the end of your trough?

Sometimes preparation is practical. Sometimes it is emotional—the sense that you are making room before you know exactly what will fill it.
