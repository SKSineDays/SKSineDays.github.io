Earth imagery: NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble: Next Generation, July 2004 global base map. Resized and mapped to a sphere; the static fallback uses the same map. Lighting is an artistic presentation, not current sunlight or weather. NASA does not endorse SineDay.

Original NASA map · NASA media guidelines

Approximate country and city positions: Natural Earth v5.1.2 public-domain map data and populated places. Springdale, Arkansas and Paris, Texas use public place positions from the U.S. Census Bureau 2025 Gazetteer. Country and territory names: ISO 3166 catalog via pycountry 26.2.16. Lights use public map positions and aggregate counts, never individual coordinates. Boundaries are a cartographic reference, not a statement about sovereignty.
