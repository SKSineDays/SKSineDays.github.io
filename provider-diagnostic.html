Preview-only provider diagnostic

This tests Google or Apple identity proof only. It never creates a SineDay account or signs you in to SineDay. It does not test age eligibility or the Supabase signup hook.

You choose whether to open a provider. The provider may show its usual sign-in or permission screen. This diagnostic checks the identity token in memory and does not write it to application logs or storage. Google or Apple may use their own cookies and account state. Only pass or fail is shown.

Available only on an explicitly configured preview. No provider starts automatically.
