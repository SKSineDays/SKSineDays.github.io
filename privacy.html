Optional globe participation

You can choose to add one light for your account to the SineDay globe. Participation is off by default. If you opt in, we save your selected country or territory and optional public city key, participation setting, consent version and time, and the record’s update time. We do not request GPS access or infer your globe location from IP address, timezone, locale, or private profiles.

Other signed-in dashboard members see approximate country or city lights derived from participating account counts and public map positions, without member names or account identifiers. A city choice is more specific than a country choice and requires updated consent. This is self-selected representation, not verified residency, guaranteed anonymity, or an indication that you are online. Email-only subscriptions do not become globe members.

Use “Manage my light” below the globe to change your country or city, return to country-only precision, or remove your light. Removal deletes the participation record and updates your local globe immediately after confirmation. Other viewers’ cached snapshots refresh within five minutes while the globe is visible. Account deletion also deletes the participation record.

Account and email eligibility checks

Our independent-account minimum is 13. We ask for the account owner’s birthdate and country of residence to evaluate eligibility. We retain the resulting age band, country, policy version, applicable minimum, decision and check time, linked to the account. The eligibility check does not retain the full birthdate. Profile birthdates separately saved for personalized calendar features are distinct from this check. Daily email setup separately derives calendar-rhythm values from the supplied birthdate rather than retaining that full date in its subscriber record.

Daily email enrollment uses a separate consent-age policy, including country-specific EU thresholds. We record its separate result rather than treating an account’s 13-or-older result as sufficient for every email subscription. These checks rely on declarations, not identity documents or verified age. We do not infer the account owner’s age from another person added to Duck Globe or a journal.

If an account or email eligibility result requires review, contact support@sineday.app to request help or correction. A review does not automatically erase existing information, stop existing email delivery, or cancel a paid subscription. We handle existing child accounts and any necessary parental authorization separately; a signup screen alone does not resolve those obligations.

Information collected

SineDay collects account information, profile/birthdate information, timezone/settings information, and technical/device data needed to operate the service, and payment-related data processed through Stripe.

Browser storage and your privacy choices

This policy covers the SineDay app and SineDay Blog. Browser storage includes local storage, session storage, and cached files, not just cookies. Select “Privacy choices” on either site to review or change optional settings. Accepting and rejecting optional uses are both available. You can use the app and read the blog without accepting optional analytics or storage.

We save your privacy choices in local storage for up to 180 days so we can respect them on later visits. Choices apply to the browser and site where you make them. The app and blog use separate origins, so a choice on one does not automatically apply to the other. Clearing site data or using another browser may cause the choice to be requested again.

  • Service storage: The app uses authentication session storage to keep you signed in, short-lived session messages to complete sign-in flows, and a local cache of account language, region, and calendar settings. Initial language and region defaults may come from your browser. Its service worker caches app files for loading and offline support. Rejecting optional uses does not turn off these service functions or sign you out.
  • Optional photo history: With your permission, the app remembers which day photos it has shown so later visits can show different pictures. Local storage holds per-day photo selection indices, not your identity, birthdate, or journal text. Session storage keeps the current selection across same-tab navigation, with a 30-minute inactivity window. Photo history remains until you clear it, withdraw this permission, or the stored data is replaced. Without permission, pictures still work without this saved history.
  • Optional affiliate referral storage: With your permission, the app remembers an Affiliate Code from a referral link for up to 30 days. Details and limits are described below.
  • Optional analytics: With your permission, the app and blog load Vercel Web Analytics to understand aggregate site use. Analytics is off until you opt in on that site.

You can withdraw any optional permission through “Privacy choices.” This stops the corresponding optional use and removes the app’s saved photo history or pending referral code when those permissions are withdrawn. Changing analytics permission may reload the page to stop the loaded analytics script. Withdrawal does not undo information already sent or delete account, payment, or completed affiliate records. You can also clear storage through your browser settings; doing so may sign you out and remove saved preferences and offline files.

Website analytics

Vercel Web Analytics does not use third-party cookies for its analytics. Even so, we ask for your permission before loading it. It processes page visits and technical information such as the page path, referring site, browser and device type, and approximate location derived from the request. Vercel describes its visitor identification as a request-derived hash with a lifespan of up to 24 hours, rather than a persistent cross-site cookie.

Our analytics configuration sends page-view events only and removes query strings and URL fragments from the page URL. We do not intentionally send birthdates, account identifiers, journal text, or Affiliate Codes as custom analytics events. For the provider’s details, see Vercel’s analytics privacy information.

How information is used

SineDay uses this information to authenticate users, provide the dashboard and premium features, generate personalized SineDay outputs, process subscriptions, support customers, and improve service reliability.

Public Daily SineDay emails

You may request personalized Daily SineDay emails without creating an app account. We use the birthdate entered during signup only to calculate the derived rhythm values needed for personalization. The public mailer does not retain your full birthdate.

We retain the email address, chosen time zone, derived rhythm values, consent record, delivery preferences, and delivery or suppression evidence needed to provide and protect the service. Pending confirmation payloads expire after 24 hours. Abuse-prevention records use keyed hashes rather than raw IP addresses and are retained for a limited period.

Daily emails begin only after you use the explicit confirmation button. Every daily email includes an unsubscribe option, and provider bounce, complaint, or suppression signals stop further delivery.

Payment processors and service providers

Payments are processed by Stripe.

Authentication and data infrastructure may use Supabase.

Transactional email services may use Resend.

Vercel provides website hosting and, when you permit it, the analytics described above. Like other web services, hosting and delivery providers receive technical request information needed to serve and protect the site.

Some app images are delivered by Unsplash, and the Supabase browser library is delivered through jsDelivr. Requests for those resources expose normal connection information, such as IP address and browser request headers, to the provider. The homepage videos are hosted as SineDay video files, rather than embedded YouTube or Vimeo players. Optional analytics settings do not block the ordinary requests needed to deliver site content.

Affiliate Program information

When a member applies to the SineDay Affiliate Program, SineDay stores their account identifier, public display name, Affiliate Code, accepted terms version and time, internal program status, Stripe connected-account identifier, and non-sensitive setup and capability statuses.

When a member applies an Affiliate Code at Stripe Checkout, SineDay stores the Affiliate Code, attribution, and attribution time. SineDay also retains commission, adjustment, payout, invoice, refund, dispute, and transfer metadata needed to operate and audit the program.

Affiliates receive aggregate performance information such as supporter counts and earnings totals. They do not receive supporter names, email addresses, user identifiers, or individual journal information.

When a visitor follows an Affiliate link and permits affiliate referral storage, SineDay stores the normalized code and capture time in that browser for up to 30 days so it can be offered during checkout. Before a choice, the code may be held only in the current page’s memory. Rejecting this storage means the code is not remembered for later visits, so referral attribution cannot be guaranteed. A visitor may still enter an Affiliate Code themselves where checkout allows it. Following a link or allowing its storage does not by itself create a completed attribution.

Affiliate payout and identity information

Stripe collects and handles an affiliate’s bank, identity, tax, payout, and available tax-document information through Stripe-hosted services. SineDay does not store an affiliate’s bank account number, Social Security number, tax identification number, identity-verification documents, or copies of tax forms.

Financial record retention

SineDay retains affiliate attribution and financial ledger records as needed for accounting, tax, fraud prevention, dispute handling, legal compliance, and support. Refunds, disputes, and corrections are recorded as reversals or adjustments rather than deleting financial history.

Apart from the optional aggregate globe representation described above, we share data with the service providers described in this policy to deliver SineDay, provide optional features you permit, or meet legal requirements.
