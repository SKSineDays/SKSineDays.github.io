SineDay in action

This 1 minute 38 second silent demonstration uses text cards and an iPhone screen recording.

  1. “Ever feel like your days are blending together?” Then: “Try journaling.” The camera moves toward the SineDay app icon.
  2. “A simple place to write about your day. Make it your own, with character.” SineDuck 17 appears, followed by the app dashboard.
  3. “Explore today’s wave. A little perspective for your day.” The Explore Wave view opens.
  4. “Create profiles for yourself and family. Explore each person’s SineDay wave.” The profile chooser opens.
  5. “A moment to remember. A thought on your mind. Your day, in your own words.” A journal entry is written and saved.
  6. “Choose the SineDuck that reflects how your day felt.” The SineDuck selection is shown.
  7. “Look back at the days you have saved. Your journal, alongside your wave.” The History view opens.
  8. “Print pages for all your profiles. Share paper pages with your family.” Printable pages are previewed. Journal, History and Printables are labeled as available with Premium.
  9. “Come see your SineDay. sineday.app.”
