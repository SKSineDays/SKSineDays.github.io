SineDay PTA / PTSA information kit

Please read before sharing

PTA rules limit commercial endorsements, so this folder uses factual, informational language. Your unit should review this referral arrangement and these materials under its state PTA and school policies before sharing.

Policies and approved arrangements vary. This is not a blanket prohibition or a statement of PTA approval. No National PTA affiliation or endorsement is implied.

Use only an active affiliate account authorized for the named group. Selecting a kit does not enroll a group, change the payout recipient or redirect an individual’s earnings. Confirm that this account’s commissions belong to the group and that the group has approved the arrangement before using its name.

Keep the commission disclosure next to every referral link. Participation is optional. Subscription payments are not donation receipts; do not promise tax deductions or earnings.
