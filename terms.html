Overview

SineDay is a digital web application that provides personalized wave-based calendar views, printable calendar and planner tools, and cloud-based notes based on a user's birthdate and the SineDay 18-day rhythm system.

Eligibility and account use

Independent SineDay accounts require the person using the account to be at least 13 years old. This is SineDay’s account minimum, not a statement that every activity or consent is available at age 13 in every country. Local rules and any required parental authorization still apply.

Our new-account check asks about the account owner, not people added to a journal or Duck Globe. It uses your declaration of birthdate and residence; it is not identity or document-based age verification. If a check requires review or needs correction, contact support@sineday.app. A review does not automatically delete saved information or cancel billing. Existing customers can still contact support and manage or cancel billing.

Daily email enrollment has a separate age and consent check. In EU countries, the threshold for independent consent may be higher than the account minimum. A mailer-only restriction does not itself suspend your SineDay account. Where independent enrollment is unavailable, contact support; contacting us does not itself establish parental consent.

The new-account age check applies to new registrations, not returning members signing into existing accounts. This does not exempt existing child accounts from applicable privacy law. We do not offer a self-service under-13 registration or verified parental-consent pathway. A parent’s relationship to the operator or an existing account is not a substitute for any required legal authorization. See Children under 13 and COPPA in our Privacy Policy, or contact support@sineday.app for help.

Users are responsible for the accuracy of profile data they enter.

Users are responsible for maintaining access to their login credentials and connected email account.
